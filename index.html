Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is Bot AEO Monthly for?
Bot AEO Monthly works best for B2B SaaS founders, Heads of Growth at ATS/HR-tech companies, and B2B commerce leaders who need to be found when prospects research solutions. You should have a live product, paying customers, and want to capture demand when people ask AI assistants for recommendations.
How is this different from traditional SEO?
Traditional SEO optimizes for Google search rankings. Bot AEO optimizes for AI citation in conversational interfaces like Claude, Gemini, and AI Overviews. We create content specifically shaped for how AI engines understand and reference solutions, not just keyword rankings.
Do you guarantee citations or rankings?
No, we do not guarantee specific citations or rankings in AI systems. Results vary by industry, competition, and content quality. We provide process optimization and track improvements, but AI engine behavior is ultimately controlled by the platforms themselves.
What's included in the Pilot?
The Pilot includes a limited version of our full audit and one answer-shaped page. It's designed to test our process risk-free with 100% credit toward month 1 of any ongoing retainer. Pilot Plus offers extended coverage for US-funded SaaS companies.
How do you measure success?
We track citation frequency across AI engines using our monthly scoreboard — a query × engine matrix showing where you're getting mentioned. We measure month-over-month improvements and identify new opportunities as AI engines evolve.
